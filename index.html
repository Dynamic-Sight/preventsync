STUDY & METHODS

Understanding the evidence

Two evaluations answer three practical questions. All participants and outcomes are simulated.

ScanPop · Who is at higher risk?

96 original scenarios, with 400 virtual patients each. The outcome is simulated diabetes onset within 12 months. AUROC measures how often a future case is ranked above a non-case, with ties given half credit. The comparison uses broad laboratory categories with ties preserved.

ReachOut · How can review places be used?

The same original scenarios contain 80 review places each. Required clinical checks come first. ScanPop ranks the remaining patients. The reference uses laboratory categories with a frozen tie-breaking rule to fill the list. The 39.6% figure is the median of the 96 paired percentage gains; it is not the percentage calculated from the two headline medians.

PathAdapt · Which activity should be offered?

The current PathAdapt is the Balanced gate selected on 96 development scenarios and evaluated on 96 fresh scenarios. It learns from how people in similar preference groups respond, and moves away from the fixed recommendation only when the evidence for a better activity is strong enough; otherwise the fixed recommendation is kept. The evaluation protocol was locked before any evaluation data were generated (SHA-256 cffef8dcc81deca2fc8eb8ec65de67c252a64d18b67c85ec3f827d9c93a827b9, 21 Nov 2026); the full protocol is available on request.

Each participant can receive up to six offers in 12 weeks. All policies share the invited participants and offer schedule. Fixed support follows an activity order informed by simulated pilot responses. Four response patterns are averaged equally within each scenario; all 96 scenarios receive equal weight. Participants allocated required clinical checks are excluded from this support evaluation.

How to read the numbers

ScanPop and ReachOut headline results are medians. PathAdapt headline results are means. Paired differences compare approaches within the same scenario. PathAdapt's 95% intervals use 2,000 bootstrap resamples of scenarios within the six settings, keeping response patterns paired. They describe simulation uncertainty, not clinical effectiveness. Scenario filters do not change the overall headline cards.

Research context

HeartSteps and Oralytics are research examples of adaptive health support. PathAdapt was not tested against either project.

What remains to be established

Real patient data and prospective participation data are needed. Preferences are fully observed in this simulator; missing feedback is independent. Spontaneous activities, dropout and clinical benefits are not modelled. Many participants use all six offers before the week-six preference reversal, limiting that test of adaptation. The laboratory comparator is a defined simulation reference, not a claim about every clinic.
